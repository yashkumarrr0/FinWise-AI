# FinWise AI

FinWise AI is a personal finance management and goal-planning application designed to help users understand their income, expenses, savings, and loans so they can plan their financial goals.

## About the Project

Users can enter their monthly income, expenses, current savings, loan/EMI details, and financial goals.

The system aims to:

* Estimate how long it may take to achieve a financial goal.
* Show the feasibility of a savings plan as Easy, Moderate, or Risky, based on defined rules.
* Categorize and analyze expenses such as food, transport, rent, and entertainment.
* Identify negative monthly cash flow and help users understand their spending shortfall.
* Show how changes in expenses affect the estimated goal timeline.

## Problem Statement

Many people struggle to track their spending, understand their available savings, and plan for future purchases. FinWise AI aims to bring expense tracking and goal planning together in one application.

## Key Features

* User authentication
* Income and expense tracking
* Category-wise expense analysis
* Loan and EMI tracking
* Financial goal calculator
* Monthly dashboard
* Cash-flow insights
